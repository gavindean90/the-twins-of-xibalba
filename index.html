A narrative poem in seven books

The Twins
of Xibalba

After the manner of Alfred, Lord Tennyson's The Lady of Shalott. Inspired by the Hero Twins of the Popol Vuh.

Two brothers. Seven books. A journey beneath the earth and beyond death.

BOOK 01

The Summons

The Hero Twins' ballgame awakens the lords below; messengers deliver the fatal summons.

 BOOK 02

The Descent

The perilous crossing of dark rivers, the four crossroads, and the trial of torch and cigar.

 BOOK 03

The Houses of Death

The ballgame of death and the trials of the Houses of Knives, Cold, Jaguars, and Bats.

 BOOK 04

The Borrowed Head

Hunahpu's head is taken; Xbalanque tricks the death lords and restores his fallen brother.

 BOOK 05

The Fire and the Resurrection

The leap into the blazing furnace, the grinding of bones, and rebirth in disguise.

 BOOK 06

The Fall of the Lords of Death

Miracles of resurrection turn upon the kings; One Death and Seven Death are destroyed.

 BOOK 07

The Ascension

The brothers leave the underworld behind and ascend to the heavens as the Sun and the Moon.

Complete edition of all seven books, spanning 140 stanzas and 1,260 verses. Dedicated to the public domain under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal.
