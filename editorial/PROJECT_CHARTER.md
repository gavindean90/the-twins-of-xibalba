# Editorial charter — The Twins of Xibalba, working edition

**Status:** Proposed editorial foundation, version 0.1 (2026-11-05). For author review, **not** a ratified rewrite policy.
**Existing published edition:** seven books, 136 nine-line stanzas. Preserve the published `book-1.md` through `book-7.md`, site and artwork unchanged.
**Episode register:** [XIBALBA_EPISODES.md](XIBALBA_EPISODES.md).
**Research authority:** [the corpus](../corpus/README.md), especially the [manuscript research dossiers](../corpus/research/README.md).

## 1. What we are making

An original English **narrative poem retelling the Xibalba-centered cycle of the K'iche' Maya Popol Vuh**, written for intelligible, memorable oral reading. The goal is that a reader unfamiliar with the myth can follow and remember its causal story, while a knowledgeable reader can see where the adaptation follows, compresses or invents.

This is **not** a translation, a comprehensive adaptation of the Popol Vuh, a claim to reproduce an ancient performance tradition or an attempt to replace K'iche' scholarship. The poem may be literary; its account of the myth should be responsibly traceable to witnesses.

The present seven-book poem is a completed first edition and a source of successful verse, not a mandatory outline for a revision. There is **no predetermined number of books**. Eleven was an exploratory count, not a constraint.

## 2. Narrative boundary and scope

The main narrative begins with **One Hunahpu and Seven Hunahpu, their family and ballgame**, not with the younger twins' summons. It continues through the first descent and defeat, the head-bearing tree, Xquic's agency and escape, the household and childhood of Hunahpu and Xbalanque, the repeated summons and second descent, the underworld ordeals, the lords' defeat, the ancestor/remembrance scene and the celestial conclusion.

The **gravitational center is Xibalba**: events aboveground should explain the actions, inherited consequences or reversal of the underworld conflict. The earlier generation and Xquic are central because they provide that causality, not because a quota of books has been reserved for them.

**Outside the primary scope:** the general creation of the cosmos and maize humanity, Seven Macaw/Zipacna/Cabrakan adventures, later K'iche' migrations, Tulan, royal dynasties and colonial genealogies. The source's four hundred youths recur at the ascension; their presence needs a consciously chosen one- or two-sentence contextual bridge or a deliberately documented omission, **not** an unplanned retelling of the entire Seven Macaw cycle. External episodes may be mentioned only where necessary to understand an in-scope scene.

The episode register is **source-story order** for the selected Xibalba cycle, not the complete order of chapters in the Popol Vuh. It is a content checklist, not yet a book-by-book outline.

## 3. Nonnegotiable narrative distinctions

- One Hunahpu is father of the younger Hero Twins; Seven Hunahpu is his brother/uncle. One Hunahpu's earlier household includes Xbaquiyalo and Hunbatz/Hunchouen. Do not silently treat the two older ballplayers as two biological fathers, or conflate the elder sons with the younger twins.
- The older ballplayers do **not** undergo the complete later series of trial houses. Their failed light/tobacco test leads to their deaths; don't invent a narrated losing ballmatch after it.
- Only **One Hunahpu's severed head** is physically identified in the tree scene. A later collective ancestral reference does not license adding a second severed head.
- Xquic is daughter of Cuchumaquic, **not** One Death. Her approach to the tree, response to its speech, negotiation with the owl messengers, substitute heart and maize test are actions with real causal consequences. Her role continues after the twins' birth; do not invent her death or disappearance.
- Keep the fruiting head/tree separate from the **later squash replacement head** of younger Hunahpu. A deliberate poetic echo is possible; an asserted identity of objects is not.
- The twins' victory **reduces the lords' power and offerings**; it does not abolish mortality or undo the original deaths completely.
- The ancestral scene offers **limited restoration and promised remembrance**, not an uncomplicated bodily resurrection.
- The manuscript's ascent clause does not explicitly name the fathers as its plural subject. Context favors the victorious younger twins, but fuller K'iche' linguistic review is pending. Neither the individual Sun/Moon assignment nor a heavenly ballgame is directly established by that short clause.

These limits are drawn from the corpus's [first ballgame](../corpus/research/first-ballgame.md), [head/tree](../corpus/research/head-in-tree.md), [Xquic conception/escape](../corpus/research/xquic-conception-and-escape.md), [Xquic/Xmucane](../corpus/research/xquic-and-xmucane.md) and [ascension](../corpus/research/ancestors-and-ascension.md) dossiers. They remain subject to documented improvement, not unrecorded override.

## 4. Source policy for a writing session

1. Locate the relevant [stable event and passage IDs](../corpus/indexes/passages.json) and read the corresponding dossier.
2. For consequential wording, distinguish the Ximénez K'iche' manuscript, its Spanish column, Scherzer, Brasseur's edited K'iche'/French, Guthrie, Spence and modern scholarship. These are not independent votes. The manuscript is itself a colonial written witness, not unmediated access to an ancient oral performance.
3. Identify evidence as **M** (selected manuscript image checked), **P** (historical printed parallels anchored, manuscript collation pending), **S** (seeded from a historical retelling or uncollated source) or **U** (unresolved). See definitions in the episode register. These grades express research coverage, not a numerical probability of truth.
4. For material graded P or S, check closer witnesses *before* publishing a definitive source claim. Neither a printed editor's parenthesis nor a modern scholar's reconstruction should masquerade as a manuscript statement.
5. Keep exact source spelling, source quotations, our new poetic phrasing and editorial commentary separate. Don't bulk import modern copyrighted translations. Cite source location, uncertainty and the adaptation choice in the eventual stanza audit.

Permitted creative additions include image, movement, dialogue transitions and controlled compression **when they do not reverse the source's event, character agency or consequence**. A substantially new event, character motivation, cosmological identification, geography or causal step is a **declared invention** requiring an editorial decision, not a silent embellishment. The goal isn't to remove invention: it is to control it.

## 5. Poetic form: preserve the identity, audit its practice

The first edition uses **nine-line stanzas** with the rhyme structure **AAAABCCCB**, with *Xibalba* terminating lines five and nine. Predominantly iambic tetrameter and a Tennysonian forward pulse are the **performance targets**. Keep this as the default design for a new edition unless the author makes a recorded change after a formal audit.

Do not force a false source detail, spurious medieval image, wrong color or vague abstraction merely to supply a rhyme. In particular, avoid relying reflexively on *stone/throne/alone*, *dead/dread* or *night/light* in place of actual events. Proper names and the refrain should be checked in spoken cadence; metrical rules are **reviewable**, not a reason to distort K'iche' names.

Each stanza should have a purpose: advance causality, distinguish a person, establish a consequential image or earn a deliberate pause. Repetition is useful for oral performance; repeated information without development should be challenged. The material is sometimes violent, but the intended voice is clear and direct rather than gratuitously lurid or generically medieval-European.

## 6. Bounded production workflow

**Pass A — Architecture:** confirm this charter and the complete in-scope episode list; decide which episodes need fully dramatized scenes and which may be compressed. **Only afterward** decide book divisions and stanza budgets.

**Pass B — Audit V1:** assign persistent stanza IDs (independent of future book numbering) and classify each stanza: **keep / revise / relocate / replace / remove**, with notes on source fidelity, narrative function, meter, rhyme and performance. Preserve admired passages, including the opening/closing Xibalba echo, without treating them as beyond review.

**Pass C — Episode drafting:** draft **one bounded episode or ~5–10 stanzas** at a time from a brief containing: this charter, the event/passage IDs, the pertinent dossier excerpt, prior accepted decisions and a small amount of neighboring verse. Do **not** load the entire corpus and whole poem into every drafting context.

**Pass D — Separate editing passes:** (1) source and continuity; (2) narrative clarity and pacing; (3) stanza form, meter and rhyme; (4) literary language and oral performance. An agent's self-evaluation is not equivalent to independent editorial review.

**Pass E — Integration:** assemble books and then the full epic, checking cumulative pacing, voice, motif, transitions and the opening/end frame. Publish only after deliberate author approval.

GitHub files are durable memory; ephemeral AI context is not. Every accepted deviation, disputed reading, book boundary or major revision should have a short decision record with evidence and rationale. The author retains final artistic control. Do not edit source texts or metadata to make verse appear more faithful.

## 7. Open author decisions (intentionally unresolved)

- Book count, boundaries and relative lengths.
- Which V1 stanzas survive an evidence-based stanza audit.
- Whether poetic dialogue for the fathers, Xquic or ancestors is acceptable when not represented as source speech.
- Whether the stars/four hundred youths receive a brief contextual mention or are omitted with an editorial note.
- Whether to name the younger twins individually as Sun and Moon, or preserve the more qualified source wording.
- How strictly individual lines must scan against the oral-performance target once properly tested aloud.

## 8. Acceptance for this phase

This charter and the episode register are **planning artifacts**. Completion here means the project's scope, exclusions, evidence hierarchy, unresolved questions and finite narrative beats are explicit and traceable to the existing corpus. It does **not** mean source collation is complete, the author has approved every choice, a final book outline exists or any new verse has been authorized.
