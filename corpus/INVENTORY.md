## Concordance coverage

21 stable episode IDs: 16 seeded narrative episodes plus five broader creation/history topics. Sixteen have verified Spence text locations; ascension also has Guthrie and Brasseur locations. Six episode records have anchored references to the existing poem. Eleven selected episodes now have manuscript image anchors, with additional Spanish, French, English and German parallels. The 17 mapped images cover 12r–18r and 31r–32v; three short readings have distinct gloss/uncertainty records. Most other manuscript folios and stanza mappings remain unmapped. The indexes expressly distinguish those states.

These counts describe files acquired and mapped, not a claim that every scan leaf or OCR word has been manually verified. See [backlog](BACKLOG.md) for the remaining work and [rights assessment](RIGHTS.md) before reuse.
