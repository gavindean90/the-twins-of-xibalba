This is a source-based planning constraint document, not a new accepted outline. It neither renumbers nor edits the seven published books. The approximate eleven-book expansion remains an editorial goal; research cannot dictate its pacing or supply four predetermined new volumes.

## Constraints on the ending

The research does not require adopting Brasseur's parenthetical fathers as the ascending pair. The younger twins remain the stronger contextual reading, subject to the linguistic review described in the ascension dossier. Nor does it support smoothing the ancestral scene into complete bodily resurrection. The promise of remembrance can carry real narrative weight without reversing the earlier loss.

The immediate ascent clause does not name which individual becomes Sun and which Moon. The existing poem's assignments should be understood as literary specificity beyond that clause. The four hundred youths' appearance also belongs to the source ending: including them requires the earlier story to have prepared their identity, while omitting them should be a deliberate documented compression.
