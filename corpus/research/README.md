## Evidence conventions

- **M** means the Newberry Ayer MS 1515 digital facsimile, `ximenez-pdf`. Foliation is read from visible annotations, whose hand/date are not established. K’iche’ is the left column; Ximénez’s Spanish is the right. This colonial transcription is a mediated witness, not unmediated ancient oral speech.
- **S** means Scherzer 1857, `scherzer-txt` / `scherzer-pdf`. Its Spanish is a printed witness, not interchangeable with every manuscript spelling or version.
- **B** means Brasseur 1861, `brasseur-txt` / `brasseur-pdf`. Even-numbered text pages are edited K’iche’, odd-numbered pages French. His chapter divisions are editorial.
- **G** means Guthrie in *The Word*, 1905–1906; issue-specific asset IDs prevent confusion with unrelated articles in the same OCR files.
- **Sp** means Spence’s 1908 retelling, `spence-txt`, not a complete translation.
- **P** means Pohorilles 1913. OCR readings remain provisional unless a specified scan region was checked.

The existing passage IDs in [passages.json](../indexes/passages.json) carry exact local line ranges and excerpt SHA-256. A verified text **location** does not make every OCR character correct. Image locators establish episode presence; three limited diplomatic excerpts have separate uncertainty and review records. Historical evidence, interpretation and future literary choices are labeled separately throughout.

No modern translation has been added to the source corpus. Research summaries are newly written. The checked manuscript material supports the dossiers’ sequences, but only the explicitly identified small excerpts are diplomatic transcriptions. Narrative summaries of the remaining K’iche’ rely on alignment with the accompanying Spanish and printed witnesses and are not offered as independently verified translations.
